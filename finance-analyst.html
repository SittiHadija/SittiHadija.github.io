Case files

Three jobs, each told as a short story: where things stood, what I was asked to do, what I did, and what changed.

PeriodJan – Apr 2026 (3-month contract) EntityPT JMMS, about 80 employees RoleAccount Receivable & Tax Staff

Turning slow collections into Rp1 billion a month

When I joined PT JMMS, collections were stuck at about Rp100–200 million a month, and many unpaid invoices were left over from earlier periods. My job was to collect both current-year and prior-year receivables.

I started by finding the PIC contact for every unpaid project, then followed up on a steady rhythm over WhatsApp and email in a corporate tone. Some messages were not landing, so I added face-to-face meetings. Alongside that I prepared VAT invoices, PPh 21 and the annual corporate tax return in Coretax.

Collections reached Rp400 million, Rp700 million and Rp1 billion in months one to three, growth of more than 300%. The mix changed too. Invoices from earlier periods fell from 70% of collections to 10%, so most of the cash coming in was from new invoices.

Tools: Coretax, WhatsApp and email follow-up

PeriodJan 2019 – Feb 2023 (about 4 years) EntityPT Hayuri Berkah Berlimpah, about 50 employees RoleManager Accounting & Tax

From manual records to a monthly cost review

The owner of PT Hayuri was focused on selling. Financial and tax records were still manual and transaction data was incomplete. I was asked to build a structured financial and tax system the owner could rely on.

I collected sales, purchase and bank statement data transaction by transaction, then built the full accounting cycle in Mekari Jurnal, from journals to the P&L and balance sheet, with tax reports on top. Once it ran, I handled bank reconciliation, cash flow control, all tax filings and a payroll calculation template. Every month I analyzed costs with Excel charts and went through them with the owner: which costs to make more efficient, and what to optimize so income kept growing.

Reporting was structured from the first year. Over my four years in the role, monthly income grew from roughly Rp200 million to Rp700 million. The organized system and the monthly cost review were my contribution to that growth.

Tools: Mekari Jurnal, Excel, Coretax

PeriodJun 2014 – Jan 2017 EntityKlinik B'Care, Mimika Baru RoleStaff Accounting

Books good enough to put in a proposal

Klinik B'Care had only patient receipts and bank statements. The owner wanted tidy financial reports to qualify for bank loans and partnerships with large companies, and I was responsible for building the bookkeeping system on my own.

I confirmed the purpose of every bank transaction with the owner, then built an Excel recording system with VLOOKUP and HLOOKUP, pivot tables and charts, running from the general journal to the P&L and balance sheet.

With orderly books, the clinic could put its financial statements, not only its business activities, into proposals to partner companies. That helped secure MoUs with several companies for routine MCU and employee health services, and monthly revenue grew from about Rp50 million to Rp200 million.

Tools: Excel (VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, pivot tables, charts)

Financial Statement Insight Analyst

Many UMKM owners watch only their cash balance, so falling margins and cash flow problems go unseen. This tool works as a digital CFO. It reads an uploaded Excel financial statement, calculates the KPIs, and writes a PDF report that flags profitability issues, cash flow problems and early warning signs.

  1. File Loader
  2. Parser
  3. KPI Calculator
  4. Prompt with Memory
  5. LLM
  6. PDF Generator
  7. Output

Tested on a synthetic dataset I built, "Kopi Nusantara": 12 months of fictional F&B UMKM data across seven sheets, designed so a healthy cash balance hides declining margins. It is still being developed and is not yet released.
