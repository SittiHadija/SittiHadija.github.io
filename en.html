About

Accounting, tax and finance, built into systems that work

I am an accounting, tax and finance professional with 8 years of experience in Accounts Receivable (AR), collections, invoicing, financial reporting, bank reconciliation, month-end closing and Indonesian tax compliance (PPh 21, PPh 23, VAT/PPN, Corporate Income Tax, Coretax DJP).

At PT JMMS, I increased monthly AR collections from roughly IDR 100–200 million to IDR 1 billion within 3 months, while shifting the collection mix from 70% overdue prior-period balances to 90% current-period invoices.

I have built accounting and finance workflow systems from scratch twice: a manual-to-structured accounting system at Klinik B'care, and a Mekari Jurnal-based accounting cycle at PT Hayuri Berkah Berlimpah, where the company owner gave me a professional recommendation.

I have since extended this workflow-design foundation into finance automation and AI-powered process automation, including an IFRS-ready accounting system prototype in Excel/Google Sheets. I currently provide freelance tax, accounting and bookkeeping services, and I am looking for full-time remote Accounting, Accounts Receivable and Tax roles.
